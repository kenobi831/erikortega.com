A BUILDER BY TRADE.
A CREATOR BY CURIOSITY.

Same person.
Different tools.

I came from Mexico to the United States and built a life the way I lay a floor: prep the base, keep it level, don’t rush the glue.

Flooring is what my hands know best. On the side, I write software — estimating tools, lead systems, and simple apps. Self-taught, one HTML file at a time.

Away from the work, I’m Santi’s dad first. My son is at the heart of it all. Usually near a grill, a record, or Sky, my dog and best pal. There’s a real life behind the screen. That’s the point.
