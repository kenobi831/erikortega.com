About me

the longer version
The story

I came up in construction and field work — jobs where you learn to read a floor, a crew and a customer before anyone tells you how. That never left. I live on a ranch outside Salinas with crop fields on every side, and I still build with my hands. More and more, I build with code too.

I'm self-taught. No CS degree, no team — a laptop, a Starlink dish and a habit of shipping. Almost everything I make follows one recipe: a single HTML file, Supabase, Netlify, no build step. If it works on a phone at a jobsite, it's done.

Husband and dad first. Asadas, family trips and teaching my son to build things matter more than any chart.
